# LeviLamina Usage Guidelines

This document is only a **translation** of the usage guidelines; the original text is the **Chinese version**. If there is any conflict between this document and the Chinese version, **the Chinese version shall prevail**. In addition, because this translated version may not be updated in a timely manner**, we recommend that you check the Chinese version to ensure that you are **aware** of updates.

## Purpose

We really enjoy seeing our users and fans do cool things and share them with the community. Our goal is to create an environment where you can make great contributions to the *LeviLamina* community while preventing others from spoiling the experience for everyone. We recognize that you may create and share some content for the community and create other content to sell. We have established these guidelines to explain how to use the *LeviLamina* name, brand, and assets in your creations.

In short, we hope these guidelines clarify what you can and cannot do, helping you create and share more while stopping those who might cross the line.

### Important Legal Agreements

[The LeviLamina End User License Agreement](https://github.com/LiteLDev/LeviLamina/blob/HEAD/EULA.en.md) (EULA) is a legal agreement between you and us ([*LeviMC*](https://github.com/LiteLDev)). The EULA covers your use of *LeviLamina* products, governs your use of our website, and defines how we resolve disputes. It is your responsibility to understand and comply with the EULA.

You should follow these guidelines alongside the EULA, though they do not form part of the EULA. They exist because we currently believe that allowing some flexibility in the use of *LeviLamina* is a good idea.

## Essential Guidelines

These are the basic guidelines for **prohibited** uses of our name, brand, and assets. You should still read all policies and guidelines in full, but here is a brief summary of the key points.

If you use any part of any name, brand, or asset, then:

**Do not** engage in any activities or include any content that might lead people to believe that the content you share is official, approved, endorsed, associated with, supported by, or related to us.

- **Do not** engage in any unlawful, misleading, obscene, harmful, or offensive activities.
- **Do not** engage in any activities that could damage or undermine our name, brand, or assets (for example: gambling, pornography, violence, terrorism, or other unsafe/adult content).
- **Do not** redistribute our products or any modifications of our products or product documentation.
- **Do not** allow unfair or unreasonable access to any content created by us.
- **Do not** impersonate *LeviMC* or appear to be associated with or supported by *LeviMC*, and explicitly state:
  - That you (rather than us) are responsible for the products or services (including events, servers, books, and handmade items).
  - Who the publisher, manufacturer, seller, organizer, and/or owner is.
  - A contact and contact information for the products, services, or any related purchases (chat and forum links are not acceptable).
- It is unwise to attempt to obtain any data that violates the [Minecraft EULA](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/eula) or could harm the security of [Minecraft](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us) by dumping [*LeviLamina*](https://github.com/LiteLDev/LeviLamina) or any of [our other products](https://github.com/orgs/LiteLDev/repositories) (see the first entry under "**Definitions**" in the [EULA](https://github.com/LiteLDev/LeviLamina/blob/HEAD/EULA.en.md) for more details)!
- **Do not** attempt to reverse-engineer *LeviLamina* for malicious purposes!
- We do **not** want you to use *LeviLamina* to develop any programs that could compromise Minecraft's security, or any other products that we consider a threat to Minecraft's security.

Include a prominent disclaimer on your products, listings, descriptions, websites/pages, and all other related materials stating: "Not an official *LeviMC* product [product/service/event/etc.]. Not approved by *LeviMC* and not associated with *LeviMC*."

These guidelines explain what we do and do not allow when you use our name, brand, and assets. We aim to be as open, honest, and, most importantly, trustworthy as possible, and we hope you will treat us the same way.

## Naming Guidelines

You may use our name in connection with your product or service, title, or listing (including on websites, video platforms, or merchandise) if you follow the guidelines in this section.

If you:

- Use the *LeviLamina* name as a secondary name, secondary title, or description because it is necessary to honestly and fairly describe your creations or their purpose.
- Ensure that the secondary title (which includes the *LeviLamina* name) is not the dominant element or distinctive part of the complete name or title.
- Do not use any other aspect of our brand or assets as part of any related branding, including as a logo or part of a logo.
- Do not use our name as keywords or search tags for products that are unrelated to it, infringing, or counterfeit.

Then you may use the *LeviLamina* name.

## Personal Use

We are very relaxed about things you create for yourself. Pretty much anything goes, so go ahead and have fun—just remember to follow the policies and **avoid any illegal activities (such as writing cheats), infringing on others' rights, or violating the Minecraft EULA.**

You are also welcome to share your creation with the community—in fact, we are excited for you and can't wait to see what you've been creating. However, sharing your content with the community (whether you plan to make money from it or not) counts as a commercial activity, which means you must follow the Commercial Use guidelines.

This applies, for example, if you set up and run any non-commercial blogs, servers, community forums, fan sites, fan clubs, news groups, events, or gatherings.

## Commercial Use

We apply stricter rules when you use our name, brand, and assets for commercial purposes. We love that you want to share your creations with the community, and we understand that you may want to charge others, but we do set limits on what we consider acceptable *LeviLamina* commercial use.

In these guidelines, commercial use means any use of our name, brand, or assets that you share with others, whether you receive payment or provide it for free. This includes videos and streams you create, merchandise you make, and even a server you host. We may change this definition over time, but for now, we hope it clarifies what you can and cannot do—helping you and other fans create more while limiting those who try to overstep.

We currently follow the commercial use guidelines in Minecraft’s usage rules.

## Summary

We love seeing members of our community do cool things, and we want to support your creativity. We understand that you may want to share your creations with others and even make money from what you have created. While we want to empower you to create and share, we also want to limit those who would take things too far. We hope these guidelines help you understand what we allow and what we do not.

If these guidelines do not cover something and we have not explicitly said it is okay, we probably do not want you to do it. In any case, if it is not covered, please do not do it without our written permission. If these guidelines, our account terms, or the [End User License Agreement](https://github.com/LiteLDev/LeviLamina/blob/HEAD/EULA.en.md) applicable to any of our games specifically cover and permit something, you do not need to contact us.
